# Hormuz AI work delivery goal

Ship a coherent paid experience for engineering teams whose AI spending and
response time keep growing: keep their supported agents, choose budget and
pace, connect ordinary work, and see the cost, time, route, coverage and actual
workflow evidence for the whole job. Hormuz makes fast decisions in the
compaction/redaction path and learns from each customer's own observed work.

This is the acceptance contract for the approved product and website lists.
Screens, documentation and provider fixtures cannot substitute for working
behavior or live qualification. Unknown outcomes remain unknown. Customer
preferences are cost, speed or quality within a budget; no subjective rating
request or universal numerical quality score is required.

## Product acceptance

1. **Connect existing tools.** Named supported agents, API protocols, exact
   tested versions, generated workspace address and guided setup lead to an
   ordinary attributed request. Show observability and enforcement coverage.
2. **Account for the whole job.** Requests, retries, failovers and answer-cache
   use join a durable owned work ID, repository and actor. Concurrent requests
   and restart uncertainty retain their reservations. Unknown attribution is
   visible rather than silently assigned.
3. **Choose budget and pace.** Workspace/repository monthly and job lifetime
   caps compose atomically. Customers choose cost, speed or quality. Pausing,
   stopping and explicitly increasing a job allowance work without escaping
   parent policy or losing existing spend.
4. **Route quickly.** Conservative task characteristics, compatibility,
   continuation affinity and customer-local observations select eligible
   routes. Insufficient evidence uses the configured baseline. Optional
   approved exploration has a bounded monthly allowance and never duplicates
   tool actions. Decisions and measured overhead are reviewable.
5. **Learn without ratings.** Explicit workflow completion conditions and
   verified GitHub/Linear object bindings ingest actual completion, failure,
   correction and reopening. Context and completion-condition versions scope
   comparisons. Missing evidence, ambiguous associations and repeat requests
   are uncertainty, not inferred correctness.
6. **Reuse safely.** Exact answer caching observes actor, context, policy,
   provider/credential generation, eligibility, freshness and invalidation.
   Corrective evidence and a bounded repeated-hit guard bypass stale answers.
   Separate provider prompt-cache usage, answer-cache hits and job completion.
7. **Show economic and timing evidence.** Reports expose whole-job cost,
   provider time, gateway overhead, retries/failures, coverage, forecasts and
   local evidence. Configured estimates and trusted provider-confirmed
   amounts remain separate. Forecast assumptions and unknown holds are shown.
8. **Operate a paid product.** Qualification, trusted checkout binding, signed
   payment activation, entitlements, cancellation, provider failure, support
   receipts, consistent backup/restore, updates and removal form one journey.
   Document the supported deployment boundary and qualify the actual release.

## Website acceptance

1. Lead with engineering teams controlling growing repository AI bills;
   retain existing hardware and other offers as secondary destinations.
2. Use “Keep your agents. Choose your budget and pace.” and an honest workflow
   discussion CTA while customer qualification is required.
3. Surface named tool/version/provider/setup compatibility and coverage early.
4. Demonstrate connection → priority/budget → ordinary task → route → limit →
   result receipt. Clearly identify fixture data and verified live evidence.
5. Publish whole-work evidence with cost/time conditions, Hormuz overhead,
   unknowns and limits. Do not invent customer savings or demand.
6. Keep compatibility confirmation → subscription → activation consistent,
   preserve approved prices, state separate provider fees and cancellation.
7. Qualify inquiry acknowledgement/delivery, booking, consented acquisition
   attribution, mobile layouts and publication. Measure private connection,
   first work, receipt opening, repeated work and verified payment separately.
   Paid acquisition begins only after this journey is qualified.

## Evidence and completion

Each requirement needs executable behavior, appropriate regression checks and
a receipt or inspected UI. The implementation belongs to branch
`mehrdad/ai-work-experience`, PR #456. Preserve predecessor contracts and the
dirty primary checkout. Rebuild evidence whenever its source fingerprint
changes, and verify the exact remote commit before reporting CI or publication.

Live gates are real provider credentials and permitted ordinary requests;
Stripe account/price/webhook/identity qualification; the deployed workspace
and HTTPS origin; actual inquiry delivery and booking; the Pages source pin
and published pages; and distributable release/update qualification. Missing
access must be reported as a specific remaining prerequisite. A test payment,
fixture response, draft PR or local browser does not close a live gate.

Do not resume or provision paid hosting before verified customer payment.
Do not charge a customer, send outreach or launch ads merely to manufacture
completion evidence. Complete all independent source and local validation
work while a live gate is pending.
