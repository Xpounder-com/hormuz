Hormuz {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is configured as a private ClusterIP service.

The chart deliberately creates no public ingress, TLS certificate, Secret,
PostgreSQL service, IdP, provider, or custody backend. Before admitting traffic,
verify the exact signed image digest and provide immutable, generation-scoped:

  configuration: {{ .Values.configuration.name }}
  runtime Secret: {{ .Values.runtimeSecret.name }}

Empty customer network-policy extensions are fail-closed. Public TLS and the
authenticated private hop remain customer-controlled. OIDC is a generic bearer
JWT resource-server contract; browser sessions are outside this profile.
