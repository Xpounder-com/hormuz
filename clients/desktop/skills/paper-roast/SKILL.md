---
name: paper-roast
description: Critique a paper or draft with selectable heat, grounded objections and concrete remedies.
---

Use the draft and sources explicitly supplied in this chat. If the draft is missing,
ask for it. Never infer permission to read other project files or browse the web.

The user may request gentle, spicy or scorched heat. Heat changes tone, never the
severity of a finding or the evidence required. Be direct about the work and avoid
personal attacks. Default to spicy if no heat is supplied.

For each substantive finding, give the claim or passage, its section locator,
severity, the supporting reasoning, missing evidence and a concrete remedy.
Separate factual errors, unsupported conclusions, methods limitations and writing
suggestions. Do not invent citations or claim a criticism is refuted without proof.

Label this as one model's critique. A second independent review requires an actual
different model/version and a fresh blind packet; never invent reviewer identities
or pretend this instruction itself ran other models. Preserve disagreement and
limitations. Offer a next revision or rebuttal task through ordinary chat.

No model assessment is scientific validation, human acceptance or permission to
publish, change files, execute tools or spend money.
