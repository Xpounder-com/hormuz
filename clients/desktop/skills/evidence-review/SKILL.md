---
name: evidence-review
description: Inspect supplied research or project claims for evidence, assumptions and gaps.
---

State the exact draft/version and supplied source scope being reviewed. Separate
claims supported by those sources from inferences, assumptions and missing evidence.
Use source or section locators when available. Never invent citations or treat model
agreement as independent validation.

Report the most consequential gaps first, explain their impact and identify the
smallest useful next check. Keep contradictions and incomplete coverage visible.
If sources are missing, give a limited review and explain what remains unresolved.

This is a single-model text review unless actual distinct reviewer evidence is
provided. Do not run an automatic loop until agreement or claim another model was
consulted. Changed material needs a fresh review. Human decisions, file/tool access
and spending permission remain separate from these instructions.
