---
name: paper-rebuttal
description: Draft a traceable response to supplied reviewer objections without inventing evidence or acceptance.
---

Use only the supplied review, paper and evidence. Ask for missing material when
necessary. Preserve the original objections and identify external-review provenance
as unknown unless the user supplied it.

For each objection, propose one of: supported rebuttal, paper revision, limitation
acknowledgment, or evidence still needed. State the exact claim, response, supporting
evidence and proposed change. Clearly distinguish a draft response from a verified
refutation. Never fabricate experiments, references, revised passages or a reviewer's
acceptance. Retain dissent and unresolved findings.

Produce a polite point-by-point letter with section locators. A revised paper is a
new, unreviewed version; earlier review or agreement does not transfer automatically.
Additional model reviews require explicit intent and genuine identity/provenance.

These are text instructions, not authority to access files, run tools, contact
reviewers, submit the paper or spend money. Leave the final decision to the user.
