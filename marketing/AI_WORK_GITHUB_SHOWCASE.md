## Candidate announcement

**Keep your agents. Choose your budget and pace.**

Hormuz's AI Work source candidate joins supported AI requests to a work record,
applies spending boundaries before dispatch, and selects eligible models using
your cost/speed/outcome priority and customer-local evidence. Exact answer reuse
stays separate from actual work completion; correction evidence can bypass it.

Try 21 OpenAI and Anthropic examples locally without a provider key. Then select
a small explicit live run with your own account, use a familiar SDK through the
gateway, and connect actual GitHub or Linear source observations.

Start with the [walkthrough](../docs/TRY_HORMUZ.md),
[provider examples](../examples/providers/README.md),
[SDK recipes](../examples/sdk/README.md) and
[workflow delivery](../docs/AI_WORK_DELIVERY_EXAMPLES.md).
The [recorded demo](https://usehormuz.github.io/demo/) shows synthetic gateway
controls and publishes its conditions. We are looking for compatibility reports
from concrete workflows; we are not claiming measured customer savings or demand.

AI Work currently requires the reviewed source candidate; published v1.8.0
installers remain separate. Providers charge for live API usage. The gateway
covers traffic routed through it, not closed apps or unrelated subscriptions.

## What to collect from a trial

Collect only a deliberately sanitized version/OS/API/scenario/status report.
Keep raw work receipts in the customer's environment. Confirm a real provider
request, an actual workflow observation and subsequent use independently. Do not
infer a paying customer from a download, star, issue, model answer or cache hit.

Public distribution is ready only after the new source is pushed, exact-commit
checks pass, and the website source pin and live links are verified. Preserve the
existing no-paid-hosting-before-verified-payment requirement.
