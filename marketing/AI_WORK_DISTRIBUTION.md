# Hormuz engineering AI work: operating distribution plan

Status: prepared, not published or sent. No campaign launch, spend, messages,
provider calls, customer performance claims, or new offer price are authorized
by this document. The approved catalog remains unchanged.

## One acquisition path

Use the engineering homepage for teams already using supported coding agents
across repositories with growing usage-based bills. The product story is:
keep the existing agents, choose spending and time priorities, and inspect the
work that consumed the budget. Exact billing tools and enforcement coverage
must be established before a customer commitment.

Show `/evidence/` before requesting setup. Functional provider-free controls
are visible now. Savings, faster completion, and customer adoption require
observations from actual customer work; synthetic amounts and first-response
latency cannot establish those claims. The work gateway is the product surface,
not a generalized consulting offering.

## Evidence-led sequence

1. Demonstrate an actual gateway execution and its downloadable provenance.
2. Connect one qualified existing agent path to the authenticated work gateway.
3. Record observed work, covered spending, attempts, elapsed time, and unknown
   outcomes under the customer's selected limits.
4. Produce a receipt the customer can inspect, including off-gateway coverage
   gaps and uncertain billing evidence.
5. Agree scope, operating capacity, and activation before sending the matching
   payment destination. Provider charges remain separate. Cloud subscription
   billing starts at checkout, not at technical activation.
6. Record repeat use, payment, renewal, and expanded qualified workflows from
   actual operating evidence. A website inquiry is not any of these outcomes.

## Channel sequence

Start with the people already describing increasing AI spending. Show the
working product and ask for a concrete supported connection rather than broad
opinions. Prepare practical exact-tool setup guides and measured work receipts
for owned channels and developer communities where promotion is permitted.
No community post, invitation, or outreach is sent automatically.

Once the focused destination, actual demo, and received-inquiry flow work,
review one bounded paid experiment. Search advertising is an intent hypothesis,
not proof of search volume or an efficient acquisition channel. ChatGPT Ads is
an optional channel; its eligible audience should be verified against the buyer.
Continue the owner's documented minimum-budget preference for the existing
campaign until a valid customer conversation creates a concrete evaluation
step. A click or CTR does not qualify. No increase is automatic.

## Measurement contract

Public events: evidence entry, AI Work entry, inquiry entry, and positively
acknowledged inquiry. Analytics and ad measurement remain independently opt-in;
QA is excluded. Forward only consented, bounded campaign labels. Never include
form descriptions, repository names, prompts, provider keys, or gateway tokens
in analytics or conversion data.

Private operating evidence: qualified supported workflow, connected workspace,
first attributed work episode, budget decision relied on, repeat use, verified
payment, and renewal. Link with an agreed private reference; do not infer a
booking from a calendar-link click or a payment from its return page.

Evaluate cost per activated qualified workspace and eventually retained paying
workspace, together with delivery effort and margin. Preserve missing attribution
and incomplete cost coverage rather than assigning them to an ad source.

## Existing commercial boundaries

Use only the current approved catalog and cancellation/support terms in
[commercial setup](COMMERCIAL_SETUP.md). Do not invent a separate paid evaluation
package or treat catalog pricing as customer willingness-to-pay evidence.
The complete controls and routing experience must be qualified for the
customer's actual tool and provider configuration before service activation.
