# Hormuz: software, Cloud, appliances, and managed sites

Hormuz places identity, model access, budgets, deterministic secret controls,
and usage evidence in supported model-request paths while employees keep their
coding clients. Provider credentials belong to the customer. Routine ledgers
retain bounded metadata rather than prompt and response bodies.

The initial buyer hypothesis is a platform or engineering lead introducing
supported coding clients under company provider accounts, with a named operator
and security reviewer. Traffic bypassing Hormuz remains outside coverage.
Demand and willingness to pay remain unvalidated.

| Offer | Price in USD |

The software remains Apache-2.0. A standalone appliance includes the SSD,
enclosure, cooling, power supply, and installed Hormuz gateway.
The onboarding bundle includes that appliance plus up to three hours of remote
setup for one provider and one supported application, initial policies,
acceptance checks, and administrator handoff. The bundle replaces the standalone
appliance price for that unit.

A managed site includes its associated Cloud workspace, device-health monitoring,
qualified updates, recovery guidance, and one hour of remote assistance per
billing month. Response target: within two business days, during Monday–Friday
9 am–5 pm America/Chicago, excluding local public holidays. Unused support time
does not roll over. Custom development, on-site work, round-the-clock response,
replacement hardware, and work beyond the allowance are excluded.

Model-provider charges, customer infrastructure, and applicable taxes are
separate. No additional Cloud subscription is charged for a managed site's
included workspace. Additional appliances within the same agreed site do not
create another site subscription; supported capacity must still be confirmed.
