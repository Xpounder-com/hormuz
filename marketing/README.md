## Definition of ready

Before amplification: all site paths work under `/hormuz/`; recordings retain
real output and provenance; the release/source/OCI distinction is visible;
contact says draft, never sent; all claims resolve to current source; downloads
render; required repository CI passes. Publishing a page does not establish
market demand, user adoption, mailbox delivery, enterprise readiness, or search
indexing.

After launch, recruit independent evaluators through the existing study and
hold qualified buyer conversations. Do not count recorded demos, stars,
downloads, or internal repeatability runs as people or customers. Revisit the
buyer hypothesis and offer using actual outcomes before buying broad traffic.
