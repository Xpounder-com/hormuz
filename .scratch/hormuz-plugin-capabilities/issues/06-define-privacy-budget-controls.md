## Question

Which privacy and budget promises can the router enforce within intercepted scope, and which remain advisory? Decide local-only behavior, explicit fallback authorization, estimate uncertainty, concurrent budget admission, in-flight requests, consent separation for history, and clear handling of work outside the connected client. Specify preview and restoration expectations without re-asking for already-authorized matching actions.
